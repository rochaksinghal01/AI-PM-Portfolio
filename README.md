## AI Prompt Engineering Library

This section documents a curated prompt engineering library for PM use cases — developed and refined through structured sessions to accelerate and sharpen key product management workflows using AI.

### Session 3 — Strategy, Research & Prioritisation

**Market Size Potential (TAM/SAM/SOM)**
An interactive, step-by-step prompt that guides structured market sizing. The AI acts as a strategy analyst: it asks for product, geography, customer segment, and sizing preferences before generating a TAM/SAM/SOM output in the format of your choice — text, table, or visual — with an executive summary tailored to the target audience (investors, product team, or executives).

**Feature & Competitive Analysis**
With just two inputs — company name and product type — this prompt auto-identifies 3–5 direct and indirect competitors, generates a feature comparison matrix (rows = features, columns = competitors), highlights gaps and differentiators, and surfaces five competitor features worth adopting. Output is kept under 200 words plus a table, immediately usable for stakeholder communication.

**User Research & Bias Detection**
A research strategist prompt that generates 8–10 tailored interview, FGD, or survey questions based on your product — then audits those questions for hidden biases (leading, assumptive, or overly complex framing), rewrites them for neutrality, and delivers a ready-to-use interview guide or downloadable survey with Likert-scale options.

**Feature Prioritisation**
Supports five frameworks — Impact vs Effort, RICE, MoSCoW, Kano, and Triangulate (all combined). The output is a CSV-ready priority table with scores, rationale, and a Now/Next/Later roadmap, along with the top three assumptions to validate. Audience-aware: output emphasis shifts based on whether you're presenting to execs (ROI), engineering (feasibility), or design (UX).

### Session 4 — Narrative, Journey & Roadmap

**Gemini Storybook — Persona Hero Narrative**
A high-emotion story prompt that takes three inputs (product/feature, persona name, and city/region) and generates a 250–400 word cinematic user story. The narrative arc follows: daily struggle → friction → tension → product as the hero → transformation → new routine. Includes regional authenticity, one line of natural dialogue, italicised inner thoughts, and three concrete product mechanisms woven into prose.

**Customer Persona Builder**
Generates a 1920×1080 PNG persona card (primary and/or secondary) with structured sections: Demographics, Psychographic, Tech Proficiency, Product-Specific insights, and outcome-oriented "What they want" statements. Also outputs a copyable JSON block. Inclusive and stereotype-free by design.

**User Journey Mapping**
Produces a complete customer journey map across five stages (Awareness → Consideration → Onboarding → Retention → Loyalty) with rows for actions, touchpoints, emotional experience, pain points with real customer voice quotes, and HMW (How Might We) opportunity statements. Includes a prioritised four-recommendation action list tied to a single metric each.

**Product Roadmap**
Takes product name, feature, duration, and any fixed dates, then produces a full Markdown roadmap with milestone gates (Alpha, Beta, UAT, GA), an ASCII Gantt swimlane chart across Engineering, Design, QA, and Marketing, and a risk register. Output is scannable, dependency-annotated, and launch-checklist-ready.

### Session 8 — LLMOps & Dashboard Design

**LLM Ops Dashboard — Frontend Prototype (Lovable)**
A detailed product design prompt for a high-fidelity, enterprise-grade LLMOps dashboard. The prototype serves three audiences — PMs, Business/Leadership, and Engineering — with dedicated tabs for Product Metrics (prompt intent, hallucination indicator), Business Metrics (cost per query, spend forecasts), Technical Metrics (P50/P95/P99 latency, error rate, token usage), and a Prompt Explorer with side-panel drill-down. Designed in a Stripe/Datadog-inspired SaaS style with clear "simulated vs real" labelling throughout.

**LLM Dashboard — Full-Stack Production Build (Claude Code)**
A production-ready LLMOps observability system prompt covering the complete architecture: LLM Middleware Layer (proxy logging every request, response, token, latency, and model metadata), Metrics Engine (quality, business, and product metrics), three audience-specific dashboard views, and real-time auto-updating metrics with <5s lag. Tech stack: FastAPI + React + Postgres. Outputs include an architecture diagram, database schema, middleware logic, and local run instructions.
