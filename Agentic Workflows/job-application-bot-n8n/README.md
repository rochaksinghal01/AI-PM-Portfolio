# Job Application Bot — n8n Workflow

> An agentic workflow that scrapes job listings, matches them against your profile, personalises your application materials, and tracks everything in a structured pipeline.

## What It Does
- Scrapes job boards for relevant listings based on your criteria
- Scores each listing against your skills and experience
- Generates a tailored cover letter and resume highlights per role
- Tracks application status in a pipeline
- Sends a daily digest of new matched roles

## How to Import
1. Import `Job_application_bot.json` into your n8n instance
2. Configure your job search criteria and profile
3. Connect to your job board sources
4. Set your digest notification channel
5. Activate

## Tech Stack
- n8n · AI for matching and personalisation · Scheduled trigger
